## Final Checks

When the evidence gates authorize an audit candidate:

1. Verify that content ends by page 8 and that any later pages contain references only.
2. Verify every PDF page uses a US Letter media box.
3. Inspect visible text and PDF metadata for author, affiliation, acknowledgement, grant, repository-owner, and path disclosures.
4. Confirm all fonts are embedded and all figures, tables, captions, references, and margins are legible.
5. Recheck the official venue page before human submission review because conference instructions can change.
