# Citation Archival Audit, 2026-08-03

## Result

The corrected bibliography contains 16 references:

- 7 archival conference or journal papers;
- 1 public format standard;
- 3 revision-pinned implementation or model artifacts; and
- 5 arXiv preprints for which no archival venue metadata was present on the
  primary record as of 2026-08-03.

The five preprints are `gupta2026`, `jackscales`, `mxformer`, `mxattention`, and
`mxfpbenchmark`. The manuscript labels each one as an arXiv preprint rather than
implying peer-reviewed publication. The machine-readable classifications and
primary URLs are in
`docs/evidence/citation_archival_audit_2026_08_03_v3.csv`.

## Method

Each bibliography entry was checked against a primary publisher, proceedings,
standard, official repository, or arXiv record. Archival forms replaced
preprints when an archival form was available. For the five remaining
preprints, the primary arXiv metadata and bounded title/author searches were
checked for proceedings or journal metadata. None was found. This audit does
not prove that no later acceptance exists; it records what the cited primary
records support on the audit date.

## Reviewer-Facing Interpretation

The earlier criticism that the bibliography obscured publication status is
resolved at source level: archival records are used where available, software
and model claims point to pinned artifacts, and current preprints are explicit.
The criticism that five references are non-archival remains a limitation of the
available literature, not a basis for assigning invented venues. These
preprints support context and nearest-work discussion; the paper's numerical
claims remain tied to repository artifacts rather than authority by citation.
